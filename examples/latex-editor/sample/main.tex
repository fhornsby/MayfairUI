\section{Some maths}
\label{sec:maths}

The area of a circle of radius $r$ is $\pi r^2$, and its circumference is
\begin{equation}
  C = 2 \pi r. \label{eq:circumference}
\end{equation}
Euler's identity ties five constants together:
\[
  e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
\]
Equation~\eqref{eq:circumference} is used in Section~\ref{sec:introduction}.
